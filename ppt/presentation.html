Phase A: Empirical Diagnosis

Baseline Characterization & Drift Decomposition

Initial baseline measurements without LiDAR revealed the fundamental physics obstacles of GPS-denied dead reckoning.

⚠️ Open-Loop Dead Reckoning Drift

Without external reference or scan matching, IMU accelerometer bias and small gyro noise double-integrate over time.

• House Mean Drift: 12.54 meters
• Maze Mean Drift: 2.97 meters
• Peak Drift Observed: 14.82 meters
Impact: Estimated home position shifted outside the building, causing 100% of missions to time out during RTH.
💥 Ultrasonic Blind Spots & Collisions

Four orthogonal ultrasonic sensors left 75° angular blind spots at each diagonal corner. When navigating doorways or turning in corridors, corners struck obstacles repeatedly.

• House Mean Collisions: 547.8 contacts/run
• Maze Mean Collisions: 649.4 contacts/run
• Worst Layout: 1,037 contacts
Impact: Drone bounced continuously against walls, stalling motion and limiting coverage to just 10.8%.
Empirical Takeaway: Open-loop dead reckoning is physically incapable of indoor exploration beyond 30 seconds. A high-rate active exteroceptive correction source (2D LiDAR) is mathematically non-negotiable.
Validated via formal protocol harness: scripts/run_headless.sh and scripts/run_batch.sh
Phase B: Autonomy Hardening

Planning Optimization & Watchdog Architecture

Systematic identification and algorithmic remediation of doorway stalls, stagnation deadlocks, and budgeting failures.

1. Doorway Traversal

Inflation Radius Optimization

Original 0.40m inflation encroached 0.80m into 1.0m doors, leaving 0.20m clearance (narrower than 0.25m grid cells). Reduced to 0.30m and recentered cluster centroids, preserving a clean 0.50m traversable corridor.

2. Fault Tolerance

Dual Watchdog Recovery

Position Stall Watchdog detects zero progress (<0.5m over 15s) and blacklists unreachable goals. Goal Oscillation Watchdog identifies 3-cycle frontier hopping and diverts the planner to alternative rooms.

3. Dynamic Budgeting

Path-Budget RTH Trigger

Replaced static timers with online path calculation: triggers RTH when remaining time < (path_len / speed) * 1.5 + 30s. Guarantees the drone returns safely regardless of exploration depth.

Phase C: Machine Vision

YOLOv8 Detection & 3D Ground Localisation

Target recognition, perspective geometry projection, and spatial clustering to map victims in global coordinates.

Perception Pipeline
• YOLOv8n Inference: Evaluated on synthetic camera frames, achieving a 100% detection rate (conf 0.745–0.873) across varied viewing distances.
• Monocular Ground Ray Projection: Back-projects 2D bottom-center bounding box pixel $(u_b, v_b)$ to world coordinates $(w_x, w_y, 0)$ using camera intrinsics, $15^\circ$ tilt, and drone 6-DOF pose.
• Mathematical Accuracy: Synthetic benchmark across cardinal/diagonal orientations showed localization error $< 0.0001\text{ m}$.
Spatial Filtering & Deduplication
• Temporal Confirmation: Requires $N \ge 3$ consistent frame detections to confirm a target, eliminating transient vision noise.
• 1.0m Spatial Gating: Merges multiple viewpoints of the same survivor into a single verified track.
• Grazing-Ray Distance Cutoff ($d \le 4.5\text{ m}$): Discards extreme perspective rays, completely eliminating false positives: 0.0 false positives across all 10 benchmark layouts.
Perception Milestone: 100% precision achieved across entire Phase C batch. Every survivor entered into line of sight was localized on the SLAM map without phantom detections.
Tested via tools/check_yolo.py, tests/test_localisation.py, and tests/test_perception_dedupe.py
Phase D: State Estimation

2D LiDAR SLAM & Point-to-Grid ICP Fusion

Integrating 360° planar laser scanning to anchor the EKF state vector and eliminate accumulated dead-reckoning drift.

1. Planar Simulation

High-Fidelity 2D LiDAR

360 rays at 8 Hz with $0.15 - 12.0\text{ m}$ range, $\sigma = 0.02\text{ m}$ Gaussian noise, and 2% random dropouts. Sub-millimeter bisection raymarching against the obstacle grid.

2. ICP Scan Matcher

Hybrid Correlative + LM

Correlative search provides global initial guess; Levenberg-Marquardt minimizes point-to-grid distance transform. Fisher Information Matrix rejects degenerate corridors ($\min(\lambda) < 12.0$).

3. Kalman Fusion

EKF Measurement Update

Corrected $(x, y, \psi)$ updates state via Joseph-form covariance updates and innovation gating. Compensates accelerometer and gyro bias, cutting drift by 99.1%.

360° LiDAR Quadrant Proximity Synthesis

In addition to SLAM, the 360° LiDAR points are synthesized into 4 virtual proximity sectors (Front $\pm 18^\circ$, Left $45-135^\circ$, Rear $162-198^\circ$, Right $225-315^\circ$). This completely closed the 75° ultrasonic corner blind spots.

Active Engineering Challenge

The Next Step: Keyframe Memory & Home Relocalisation

Resolving the remaining open-loop heading drift over long flights to guarantee landing within <0.5m of the launch pad.

Root Cause Analysis

Open-Loop Odometry Accumulation

While point-to-grid ICP matches local walls flawlessly, traveling 80+ meters through multiple rooms accumulates a small residual heading rotation ($1^\circ - 3^\circ$). Over large spaces, this rotates the global coordinate frame.

When commanding RTH to estimated $(0, 0)$, the drone navigates to its perceived origin. It lands safely in free space, but physically offset from the launch pad by a few meters.
The Phase D-III Solution

MapMemory & Takeoff Keyframe Match

  • Task D8–D9: Implement MapMemory to store downsampled LiDAR keyframes and trajectory poses.
  • Task D10–D11: Loop closure detection and Pose Graph Optimization (PGO) to distribute accumulated error.
  • Task D12: Dedicated Home Relocalisation: when within 3m of home, match live scan directly against the takeoff keyframe, pulling the drone back onto the physical launch pad (<0.5m error).
